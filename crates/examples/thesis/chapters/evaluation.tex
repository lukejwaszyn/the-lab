\chapter{Evaluation}
\label{chap:evaluation}

This chapter reports rebuild latency for three documents. The numbers are
illustrative placeholders for the sample project.

\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

Each document was edited at a random position and rebuilt, once with a full
run and once with the prototype described in Chapter~\ref{chap:design}.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:latency} lists the median latency over 50 edits. The
incremental rebuild is faster in every case, and the gap widens as the
document grows, which matches Equation~\ref{eq:expected-pages}.

\begin{table}[ht]
  \centering
  \caption{Median rebuild latency in milliseconds (illustrative).}
  \label{tab:latency}
  \begin{tabular}{lrrr}
    \toprule
    Document & Pages & Full run & Incremental \\
    \midrule
    Article  &    12 &      410 &          95 \\
    Thesis   &    96 &     2350 &         140 \\
    Book     &   480 &    11800 &         210 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Latency for the incremental rebuild depends mostly on the distance to the
nearest snapshot (Section~\ref{sec:snapshot-points}), not on the length of
the document.
